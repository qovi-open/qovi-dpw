# Security policy — Qovi DpW

Please **do not** open a public issue for a security problem.

Report vulnerabilities privately to **security@qovi.ch**, with:

- the version of Qovi DpW (Windows, Android or Linux) and of the operating system;
- a description of the problem and, if possible, the steps to reproduce it;
- your contact details if you want us to keep you informed.

We acknowledge reports as quickly as we can and keep the reporter informed
until a fix is published. Please give us reasonable time to correct the
problem before any public disclosure.

---

**Français.** Merci de ne **pas** ouvrir de ticket public pour un problème de
sécurité : signalez-le en privé à **security@qovi.ch**, avec la version de
Qovi DpW, le système utilisé et la façon de reproduire le problème.
